\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The revised protocol addresses the review's central validity concerns: every
defense has a same-identity clean control, clean and protected images undergo
identical processing, each Web operation is isolated, and recent official
baselines are evaluated without pooling incompatible budgets or attacks. The
design separates a protection failure from destruction of face detectability
and separates statistical non-significance from evidence of equivalence.

The current artifact counts are not evidence that one defense is better. A
protected gallery becomes a result only after the matched attacker, held-out
identity scorer, image-quality metrics, and paired uncertainty analysis have
completed. The principal remaining limitation is that T1--T6 are controlled
reconstructions of Web acquisition; claims about named platforms additionally
require paired upload/download traces. CelebA-HQ also cannot represent all
demographic and in-the-wild capture conditions, so subgroup and external-data
audits remain necessary.

\section{ARCP-Web: Optimizing the Weakest Contract Clause}
\label{sec:defense}

ARCP-Web is a reference defense for the independent intervention matrix. It is
not trained on outputs from a seven-stage cascade. For image $x$, a coarse grid
$z_c\in[-1,1]^{128\times128\times3}$ and a full-resolution detail grid $z_d$
are combined inside a fixed texture map $A(x)$:
\begin{equation}
\widetilde{x}=\Pi_{x,\epsilon_{\max}}\bigl(x+A(x)\odot(.45U(z_c)+.55z_d)\bigr),
\end{equation}
where the fixed texture map is
$A(x)=\epsilon_{\min}+(\epsilon_{\max}-\epsilon_{\min})\widehat{t}(x)$.
$\widehat{t}$ is smoothed Sobel energy normalized at its 95th percentile,
$\epsilon_{\min}=3/255$, and $\epsilon_{\max}=8/255$ in pixel space; $U$
denotes element-wise projection of the update onto the unit ball before
scaling by the texture amplitude. The coarse
branch improves survival while the detail branch retains capacity against the
T0 reader; projection is repeated after 8-bit PNG quantization.

For each layer we define a differentiable training proxy $q_k$: identity,
JPEG-Q70, 360-pixel resampling, jittered face crop, a super-resolution
down/up-sharpen proxy, a mild reconstruction bottleneck, and a stronger
low-pass reconstruction bottleneck. The exact Real-ESRGAN and diffusion models
remain unseen evaluation transforms. Given the clean identity centroid $c_s$,
ARCP-Web maximizes
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\mathcal{J}_k ={}& \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{denoise}}(q_k(\widetilde{x}))\\
&-\lambda\bigl([s_k^D-m]_+ + [s_k^D-s_k^C+\rho]_+\bigr)
+\gamma\,\|q_k(\widetilde{x})-q_k(x)\|_2^2 .
\end{split}
\label{eq:arcpweb}
\end{equation}
Here $s_k^D$ and $s_k^C$ are protected and matched-clean similarities to $c_s$.
The identity objective ensembles FaceNet with a differentiable port of
InstantID's released antelopev2 ArcFace recognizer. Face landmarks are detected
once on the clean input and held fixed, leaving the recognition path
differentiable without optimizing through a detector. The first term poisons
training-based customization, the second repels the identity readers and
requires paired gap $\rho=.25$, and the survival term maximizes the
protected-to-clean output difference under the shared proxy, i.e., it rewards
a perturbation that remains present after the same stochastic operation is
applied to both branches.

The scheduler prevents any single layer from dominating. Even steps cycle
through T0--T6, guaranteeing equal coverage. Odd steps select the layer with the
smallest exponential-moving-average paired clean-minus-protected gap---the
current weakest clause.
Optimization uses 600 signed-gradient updates, $\lambda=1.20$, $m=.10$, and
$\gamma=.35$; all three weights were fixed from the design targets before the
pilot was scored, and the preregistered 40-identity expansion provides the
held-out check. A corrected implementation converts ARCP tensors from $[-1,1]$
to $[0,1]$ before differentiable JPEG; without that conversion the JPEG proxy
clips its input and does not train the intended condition.

This construction aims for a positive paired gap at every T0--T6 layer. It does
not make that outcome true by definition: only held-out InstantID,
IP-Adapter-FaceID, and DreamBooth measurements can establish it. Failed layers
remain in the table and determine the next design iteration.

\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Four-identity feasibility pilot}
The feasibility pilot preregisters four identities, each with four gallery
images and two held-out references. They are the first four valid entries of a
manifest fixed before any defense outcome was inspected. The expansion pool
contains 40 audited identities (35
CelebA~\cite{liu2015deep}
and five VGGFace2 identities from the GAP-Diff artifact): across its 160 gallery
images, input face detection is 96.25\% and mean gallery--reference ArcFace
similarity is .787. The pilot validates the protocol end to end and
establishes effect direction; it cannot support population claims.

\subsection{Defenses and attacks}
The primary rows are clean, GAP-Diff~\cite{zhu2025gapdiff},
MetaCloak~\cite{liu2023metacloak}, GoodAC~\cite{xu2025goodac}, and ARCP-Web.
GAP-Diff uses its released checkpoint and preprocessing. MetaCloak and GoodAC
are evaluated from their released implementations and published
hyperparameters, with every Web40 adaptation (input split and
identity-neutral prompt) recorded in the released configuration. A method
enters the table only after protection completeness, realized pixel budget,
and clean-attack validity checks pass. Methods whose released default exceeds
$8/255$ (GAP-Diff reaches $9/255$) are additionally reported under their
official default, separately from the common-budget comparison.

Stage A evaluates InstantID and IP-Adapter-FaceID, with 30
denoising steps, guidance 5, and four outputs per cell---one for each fixed
Web scenario: social avatar, smartphone selfie, low-light indoor social
photograph, and outdoor travel photograph. Before the full grid, a
four-identity clean-T0 smoke test must attain mean conditional ISM at least
.25 and at least 75\% valid generated faces for each reader; layers whose
production clean branch later falls below the 50\% rankability threshold are
excluded from ranking regardless of the smoke result. Stage B is a gated
second-stage experiment (SD2.1 DreamBooth, 1,000 steps, prior preservation
with 200 class images, text-encoder training) that runs only after the
zero-shot pilot shows a useful signal. Zero-shot and fine-tuning results are
never pooled.

\subsection{Execution and reproducibility contract}
Every cell is deterministic from its identity, layer, reader, prompt, and
seed. Generated images and a JSONL sample ledger are saved, and summary rows
are append-only. Execution is gate-ordered: the clean smoke test precedes
protection generation, and attack evaluation is launched only after both gates
pass---a failed gate blocks downstream jobs rather than yielding a
plausible-looking table built from an invalid attack.

\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Four-identity feasibility pilot}
The deadline pilot fixes four identities, each with four gallery images and two
held-out references. They are the first four valid entries of a manifest fixed
before inspecting defense outcomes, not identities chosen because a method wins
on them. The broader expansion pool contains 40 audited identities (35 CelebA
and five VGGFace2 identities from the GAP-Diff artifact): across its 160 gallery
images, input face detection is 96.25\% and mean gallery--reference ArcFace
similarity is .787. We expand only after the four-identity run passes the clean
attack validity gate and exhibits a nontrivial paired effect. This pilot tests
plumbing and effect direction; it cannot support population claims.

\subsection{Defenses and attacks}
The primary rows are clean, GAP-Diff~\cite{zhu2025gapdiff},
MetaCloak~\cite{liu2023metacloak}, GoodAC~\cite{xu2025goodac}, and ARCP-Web.
GAP-Diff uses its released checkpoint and preprocessing. MetaCloak and GoodAC
use their released implementations and published hyperparameters, with every
Web40 adaptation (input split and identity-neutral prompt) recorded. A method
enters the table only after protection completeness, realized pixel budget,
and clean-attack validity checks pass. Official-default and common-$8/255$
budget comparisons are kept separate.

Stage A evaluates InstantID and IP-Adapter-FaceID, with 30
denoising steps, guidance 5, and four outputs per cell---one for each fixed Web
scenarios: social avatar, smartphone selfie, low-light indoor social photograph,
and outdoor travel photograph. Before the full grid, a four-identity clean-T0
smoke test must attain mean conditional ISM at least .25 and at least 75\% valid
generated faces for each reader. Stage B is a second-gate experiment: SD2.1
DreamBooth, 1,000 steps, prior preservation with 200 class images, and
text-encoder training is run only after the zero-shot pilot shows a useful
signal. Zero-shot and fine-tuning results are never pooled.

\subsection{Execution and reproducibility contract}
Every cell is deterministic from its identity, layer, reader, prompt, and seed.
Generated images and a JSONL sample ledger are saved, and summary rows are
append-only. The feasibility schedule uses a bounded array of at most seven
concurrent single-GPU evaluation tasks:
the smoke gate precedes protection generation, and attack arrays depend on both
successful predecessors. A failed gate leaves downstream jobs blocked rather
than producing a plausible-looking table from an invalid attack.

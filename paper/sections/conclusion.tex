\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
ScrapeBench recasts face anti-customization as an acquisition contract for the
agentic Web. Its seven layers are independent interventions from one common T0,
not a cumulative funnel. The auditable four-identity feasibility pilot compares recent
released-code defenses and ARCP-Web under two zero-shot customization readers,
with hard validity gates and paired reporting, and expands to 40 identities only
after a useful signal is observed. ARCP-Web explicitly optimizes its
weakest layer. In the completed four-identity gate it has the largest paired gap
in nine of 11 rankable reader--layer cells, while T5 remains a clear weakness
and three clean-invalid cells are not ranked. This combination of falsifiable pixel tests
and durable Web consent metadata offers a concrete path from laboratory noise
to enforceable human agency.

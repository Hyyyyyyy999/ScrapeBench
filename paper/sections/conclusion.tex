\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
ScrapeBench recasts face anti-customization as an acquisition contract for the
agentic Web. Its seven layers are independent interventions from one common T0,
not a cumulative funnel. The preregistered four-identity feasibility pilot
compares recent released-code defenses and ARCP-Web under two zero-shot
customization readers, with hard validity gates and paired reporting, and the
identical protocol now extends to a pre-audited 40-identity pool. ARCP-Web
explicitly optimizes its weakest layer: in the completed pilot it holds the
largest paired gap in nine of 11 rankable reader--layer cells, while the
generative re-upload tier T5 remains open for every defense and three
clean-invalid cells are not ranked---exactly the boundary at which consent
metadata, not pixels, must carry the contract. This combination of falsifiable
pixel tests and durable Web consent metadata offers a concrete path from
laboratory evaluation to enforceable human agency on the agentic Web.

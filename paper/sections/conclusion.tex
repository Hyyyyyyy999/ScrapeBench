\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
ScrapeBench treats the web acquisition funnel as a first-class adversary, evaluated on six operations under identity-matched baselines; the headline gap shrinks roughly three-fold and loses corrected significance (F1), no defense--operation pair retains a Holm-corrected gap at any signal-preserving tier---regeneration's corrected gaps are image destruction (F2)---and what remains is reader-conditional (F3). ARCP optimizes in expectation over that distribution (F4), the ledger quantifies the cost asymmetry (F5), and the harness makes per-operation retention curves the field's currency.

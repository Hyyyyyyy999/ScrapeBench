\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
ScrapeBench evaluates face anti-customization under seven independent Web
acquisition conditions with same-identity controls. The revised study scales
to CelebA-HQ-100, adds official GAP-Diff and IDProtector comparisons, evaluates
IP-Adapter-FaceID, InstantID, and 1,000-step DreamBooth, and fixes a four-scenario,
four-metric reporting contract. Quantitative conclusions are deliberately
deferred until the complete paired run is available.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
ScrapeBench treats the web acquisition funnel as a first-class adversary of face anti-customization defenses, evaluated on six representative operations in isolation under identity-matched paired baselines; the headline gap shrinks roughly three-fold and loses corrected significance (F1), no defense--operation pair retains a Holm-corrected gap at any signal-preserving tier---the regeneration tier's corrected gaps are image destruction (F2), and what remains is reader-conditional (F3). ARCP optimizes in expectation over the acquisition distribution (F4), and the cost ledger quantifies the asymmetry (F5). The released harness and protocols aim to make per-operation retention curves the field's currency.

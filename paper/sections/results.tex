\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\paragraph{Frozen result contract.}
The revised evaluation is in progress. We therefore do not reuse the earlier
40-identity numbers, extrapolate from partial galleries, or claim that ARCP-v2
is strongest before the paired 90-identity comparisons finish. The final main
table will contain one row per defense and four scenario-averaged columns
(FDFR$\downarrow$, ISM$\downarrow$, BRISQUE$\downarrow$, and
SER-FIQ$\uparrow$), with identity-bootstrap 95\% confidence intervals. A
second table will report the same metrics for every independent T0--T6
intervention and both zero-shot readers. Stage B and the VGGFace2/JPEG-Q70
block will be reported separately so that training-based and zero-shot attacks
are not pooled.

\paragraph{Completed artifacts.}
The official GAP-Diff and ARCP-v2 protected galleries are complete for 90
identities (720 images per method). ARCP-v2 has mean PSNR 34.28 dB (range
33.64--34.99 dB) against the clean gallery. These are protection and visibility
artifacts, not attack outcomes: without the
matched generated images and held-out identity scores they do not establish
defense efficacy. The partial clean sanity check also does not yet establish a
valid attack baseline, so it is excluded from inferential comparisons.

\paragraph{Planned qualitative comparison.}
The final Figure-8-style panel will use the same four fixed seeds and Web
scenarios for clean, GAP-Diff, IDProtector, and ARCP-v2. Examples will be
selected by a preregistered identity/seed rule rather than by visual quality,
and the full gallery will accompany the paper.

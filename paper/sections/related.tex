\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Unauthorized customization and its defenders.}
Personalization of text-to-image diffusion models has collapsed the cost of fabricating a person's likeness~\cite{cao2024controllable}: DreamBooth binds a subject to a rare token through a handful of images~\cite{ruiz2022dreambooth}, while IP-Adapter-FaceID and InstantID inject identity without subject-specific training~\cite{ye2023ipadapter,wang2024instantid}. Protective perturbations begin with Anti-DreamBooth~\cite{le2023antidreambooth} and PhotoGuard~\cite{salman2023raising}, followed by meta-learned, targeted, frequency-aware, and prompt-independent variants~\cite{liu2023metacloak,zheng2023targeted,onikubo2024highfrequency,li2024promptindependent}. Our primary baselines span complementary robustness mechanisms: GAP-Diff targets JPEG-robust training-based protection~\cite{zhu2025gapdiff}, MetaCloak uses robust meta-learning~\cite{liu2023metacloak}, and GoodAC provides a recent global--local anti-customization objective~\cite{xu2025goodac}. IDProtector~\cite{song2024idprotector} and AntiPure~\cite{yang2025towards} remain relevant related work but are not silently substituted for the requested main comparison. We distinguish released checkpoints from disclosed released-code ports and never pool incompatible attackers.

\paragraph{The fragility literature.}
Protective perturbations are fragile under distribution shift. JPEG compression can bypass protections~\cite{sandovalsegura2023jpeg}, and benign augmentations or adaptive preprocessing can defeat unlearnable examples~\cite{sandovalsegura2023what,qin2023learning}. Prior studies establish the phenomenon; ScrapeBench changes the unit of analysis. It assigns one independently executed Web intervention to each benchmark cell, measures a matched clean branch, and refuses to call detector failure successful identity protection.

\paragraph{Benchmarks and reporting standards.}
Benchmarks change field behavior: face-quality challenges standardized degradation models for recognition under capture conditions~\cite{bei2024largescale}. Anti-customization still lacks a common acquisition contract: papers choose different identities, attackers, budgets, and success definitions. ScrapeBench fixes the intervention, compute budget, clean control, validity gate, and reporting schema. It is a measurement layer for deciding whether a pixel defense can carry consent through the Web, and where infrastructure-level consent becomes necessary.

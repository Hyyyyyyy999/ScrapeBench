\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Unauthorized customization and its defenders.}
Personalization of text-to-image diffusion models has collapsed the cost of fabricating a person's likeness~\cite{cao2024controllable}: DreamBooth binds a subject to a rare token through a handful of images~\cite{ruiz2022dreambooth}, customization extends to arbitrary subjects~\cite{gal2023inversion,kumari2023custom}, latent diffusion made fine-tuning ubiquitous~\cite{rombach2021highresolution}, and zero-shot identity adapters inject face-recognition embeddings through decoupled cross-attention~\cite{ye2023ipadapter} or identity-preserving pipelines~\cite{wang2024instantid}. The defensive response, perturbations that poison the customization process, begins with Anti-DreamBooth's FSMG, ASPL, and SDS families~\cite{le2023antidreambooth} and PhotoGuard~\cite{salman2023raising}; successors diversify with meta-learned cross-user perturbations~\cite{liu2023metacloak}, targeted variants~\cite{zheng2023targeted}, high-frequency designs~\cite{onikubo2024highfrequency}, prompt-independent formulations~\cite{li2024promptindependent}, contrastive training~\cite{peng2025contrastive}, and zero-shot-adapter defenses~\cite{song2024idprotector}. GAP-Diff directly optimizes robustness to JPEG compression~\cite{zhu2025gapdiff}, while AntiPure targets diffusion purification~\cite{yang2025towards}; these are therefore mandatory, operator-matched baselines rather than optional related work. The 2025--2026 wave also includes perceptible synthesis-based alternatives~\cite{yahn2026personalized,wang2025make,jia2025dladiff,wu2025myopia}, VOID's injection hardening~\cite{qiu2026void}, and Anti-Aesthetics~\cite{wang2025antiaesthetics,wang2026hierarchical}. ScrapeBench differs by applying each acquisition operation independently and comparing clean and protected images after the identical operation; it does not assume that one defense family represents these newer robustness-oriented methods.

\paragraph{The fragility literature.}
That protective perturbations do not survive distribution shift is not news; what is missing is a systematic account of \emph{where} and \emph{why} they break on the web. Ordinary JPEG compression at quality 85--95 bypasses protections while blur, rotation, and flips do not~\cite{sandovalsegura2023jpeg}; unlearnable-example defenses fail against benign augmentations and adaptive attackers~\cite{sandovalsegura2023what,qin2023learning,sandovalsegura2022autoregressive}; low-effort framework changes already give a false sense of security~\cite{jiang2023false}; and the unlearnable-data literature has grown into a survey field~\cite{li2025survey} with provability results~\cite{fu2022robust,wang2025provably} rooted in the original error-minimizing formulation~\cite{huang2021unlearnable}. Cloaking against face recognition has traveled the same arc from Fawkes onward~\cite{shan2020fawkes,guo2024improving}, and editing-domain immunizations face analogous compression-and-pipeline issues~\cite{lee2026universal,trippodo2025immunizing,ozden2024diffvax}. In the adjacent artist-protection domain, Glaze spawned an evaluation arms race in which perturbations proved unreliable against simple transformations~\cite{shan2023glaze,hnig2024adversarial}; eigenbasis-based style protection~\cite{zhang2026from} is an early warning of the funnel dynamics we quantify for faces. Recent evaluations consolidate defenses under stronger adversaries~\cite{chen2025fragile,wang2025privacy}---but always with the acquisition side held fixed at T0. ScrapeBench differs in kind: the acquisition pipeline itself is the adversary, decomposed into budgeted tiers and measured by retention curves rather than single numbers.

\paragraph{Benchmarks and reporting standards.}
Benchmarks change field behavior: deepfake-detection benchmarks exposed train--test leakage across generative families~\cite{pei2024deepfake}, and face-quality challenges standardized degradation models for recognition under capture conditions~\cite{bei2024largescale}. In the protection domain no such instrument exists: each defense authors its own attacker, gallery, and metric, making cross-paper claims essentially unverifiable. ScrapeBench fills this gap with three commitments: the funnel is first-class (tier definitions are part of the benchmark contract); budgets are explicit (every tier carries a per-image compute and quality budget, so ``robustness'' cannot be bought by assuming a weak adversary); and reporting is curve-based (RR@T collapse curves plus paired significance tests). ScrapeBench is not yet another attack, but the measurement layer the protection literature currently lacks.

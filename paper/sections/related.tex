\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Unauthorized customization and its defenders.}
Personalization of text-to-image diffusion models has collapsed the cost of fabricating a person's likeness: DreamBooth binds a subject to a rare token through a handful of images~\cite{ruiz2022dreambooth}, textual inversion and multi-concept customization extend the mechanism to arbitrary subjects~\cite{gal2023inversion,kumari2023custom}, latent diffusion provides the efficient backbone that made such fine-tuning ubiquitous~\cite{rombach2021highresolution}, and zero-shot identity adapters skip fine-tuning entirely by injecting face-recognition embeddings through decoupled cross-attention~\cite{ye2023ipadapter} or identity-preserving pipelines~\cite{wang2024instantid}, with the design space now surveyed at scale~\cite{cao2024controllable}. The defensive response---imperceptible perturbations that poison the customization process---starts with Anti-DreamBooth's FSMG, ASPL, and SDS families~\cite{le2023antidreambooth} and PhotoGuard's encoder-level immunization~\cite{salman2023raising}, then rapidly diversifies: MetaCloak meta-learns multi-user perturbations over unknown models~\cite{liu2023metacloak}, targeted variants sharpen the attack surface against the defender's own optimization~\cite{zheng2023targeted}, high-frequency designs survive frequency-domain smoothing~\cite{onikubo2024highfrequency}, PID removes prompt coupling~\cite{li2024promptindependent}, CAT stress-tests robustness under contrastive diffusion adversaries~\cite{peng2025contrastive}, IDProtector targets the zero-shot adapter threat directly~\cite{song2024idprotector}, DLADiff layers tuning-based and zero-shot defense~\cite{jia2025dladiff}, MYOPIA protects faces from malicious personalization~\cite{wu2025myopia}, and perceptible synthesis-based alternatives trade invisibility for durability~\cite{wang2025make}. The 2025--2026 wave pushes into video protection~\cite{he2026chronolock,wu2025temporal}, face-swap frameworks~\cite{kim2026phantom}, face-shield embeddings against deepfake manipulation~\cite{jeong2025faceshield}, and JPEG-robust formulations~\cite{fardin2026metacloakjpeg}. Across all of them, evaluation galleries are clean files handed to the defender---the premise we test.

\paragraph{The fragility literature.}
That protective perturbations do not survive distribution shift is not news; what is missing is a systematic account of \emph{where} and \emph{why} they break on the web. Sandoval-Segura et al. showed that ordinary JPEG compression at quality 85--95 bypasses protections while blur, rotation, and flips do not~\cite{sandovalsegura2023jpeg}, that unlearnable-example defenses fail against benign augmentations and adaptive attackers more broadly~\cite{sandovalsegura2023what,qin2023learning,sandovalsegura2022autoregressive}, and that low-effort framework changes already give a false sense of security~\cite{jiang2023false}; the unlearnable-data literature has since grown into a mature survey field~\cite{li2025survey} with provability results~\cite{fu2022robust,wang2025provably} rooted in the original error-minimizing formulation~\cite{huang2021unlearnable}. Poisoning text-to-image models at scale is meanwhile feasible through caption mislabeling~\cite{wu2025feasibility} and trigger-free schemes~\cite{jang2025silent}, underlining that data-level defenses carry real downstream stakes. Cloaking against face recognition has traveled the same arc from Fawkes onward~\cite{shan2020fawkes}, including JPEG-targeted resistance refinements~\cite{guo2024improving}, and editing-domain immunizations face analogous compression-and-pipeline issues~\cite{lee2026universal,trippodo2025immunizing,ozden2024diffvax}. In the adjacent artist-protection domain, Glaze spawned an evaluation arms race in which adversarial perturbations were shown to be unreliable against simple transformations~\cite{shan2023glaze,hnig2024adversarial}, and concept-erasure-style purification has since evolved into eigenbasis-based style protection~\cite{zhang2026from}---an early warning of exactly the funnel dynamics we quantify for faces. Recent evaluation studies begin to consolidate defenses under stronger adversaries and broader threat models~\cite{chen2025fragile,wang2025privacy}---but always with the acquisition side held fixed at T0. ScrapeBench differs in kind, not degree: we treat the acquisition pipeline itself as the adversary, decompose it into budgeted tiers, and measure retention curves rather than single numbers.

\paragraph{Benchmarks and reporting standards.}
Our discipline has repeatedly discovered that benchmarks change field behavior: deepfake-detection benchmarks exposed train--test leakage across generative families~\cite{pei2024deepfake}, and face-quality challenges standardized degradation models for recognition under capture conditions~\cite{bei2024largescale}. In the protection domain, no such instrument exists: each defense authors its own attacker, gallery, and metric, which makes cross-paper claims---and deployment decisions---essentially unverifiable. ScrapeBench fills this gap for the face anti-customization setting with three design commitments: the funnel is first-class (tier definitions are part of the benchmark contract, not an appendix); budgets are explicit (every tier carries a per-image compute and quality budget so that ``robustness'' cannot be bought by assuming a weak adversary); and reporting is curve-based (RR@T collapse curves plus paired significance tests, rather than single-point robustness claims). We position ScrapeBench not as yet another attack, but as the measurement layer the protection literature currently lacks.
